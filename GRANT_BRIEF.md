# LTP Grant Brief

## Project summary

LTP is a protocol for deterministic replay, traceable branching, and audit-friendly evidence in systems that need continuity across tools, models, and services.

## Why this fits a commons fund

- It is open infrastructure for transparency and verification.
- It improves interoperability by defining a stable protocol surface.
- It helps teams prove what happened instead of trusting opaque logs.
- It is useful across many downstream applications, not just one product.

## Core deliverables to highlight

- stable protocol and canonical flow documentation
- conformance tooling and verification reports
- replay and inspection utilities
- SDK and adapter surfaces for multiple environments

## Grant-ready framing

Present LTP as a public-good infrastructure layer for trustworthy automation. The emphasis should be on verifiability, interoperability, and evidence generation rather than on product-style feature growth.
